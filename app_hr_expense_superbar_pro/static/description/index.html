HR Expense Superbar Pro

Browse expense reports by department and analytic account on top of Expense Enhance

Together with Expense Enhance, bring department and analytic fields into expense reports, then review them with tree navigation.

Odoo 20Enterprise & CommunityMulti-CompanyMulti-Language

Overview

The Pro edition builds on app_hr_expense_pro (Expense Enhance), so expense reports carry department and analytic account fields and can be grouped by organization or project. The tree navigator works in list, kanban, pivot and graph views with multi-select, child-node control and multi-field search, fitting companies that account costs by cost center.
