Overview

Moving from Odoo 18 to 20 is hardest for partner and user ids, xml_ids and hierarchy. This module runs on the Odoo 20 target and pulls from the live Odoo 18 over read-only XML-RPC. Matching goes xml_id first, then a record map, then business key, so repeated runs never duplicate records. Original ids and xml_ids are preserved and conflicts are logged rather than silently overwritten. depends_on drives topological ordering, parent_id is imported in two phases, and write_date + id form the incremental waterline with a 30 second overlap. Dry-run, per-record logs, a retry queue and reconciliation reports are included.
