Overview

Adds the Superbar navigation sidebar to Odoo CRM. Opportunities can be filtered by stage and sales team, leads by campaign, medium and source, and CRM reports can be analysed by several dimensions in pivot and graph views.

Provides a powerful sidebar superbar for the Odoo CRM module. Filter opportunities by stage and team, leads by campaign, medium and source, with pivot and graph report navigation.
