Overview

This module adds a powerful sidebar superbar to the Odoo CRM app. Filter opportunities by stage and team, leads by campaign, medium and source, and analyze CRM reports with pivot and graph navigation across multiple dimensions.

Integrates with UTM campaign tracking for full lead-source analysis.
