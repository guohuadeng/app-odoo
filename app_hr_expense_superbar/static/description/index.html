HR Expense Superbar

Browse expense reports by department or analytic account in list, kanban, pivot and graph views

Turn expense reports into an organization or analytic tree; click a node to filter documents, with multi-select and child-node control.

Odoo 20Enterprise & CommunityMulti-CompanyMulti-Language

Overview

This module adds a tree navigator to the left of expense list, kanban, pivot and graph views. Browse expense reports by department hierarchy or analytic account, multi-select nodes with Ctrl/Cmd, toggle whether child nodes are included, search across several fields and move the panel left or right. Useful for reviewing costs by cost center, project or department.
