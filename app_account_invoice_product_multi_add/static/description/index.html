Overview

This module adds one-click batch product entry to Customer Invoices and Vendor Bills. Click the "Multi Add Items" button to open a popup, filter and group products by category, select multiple products, and add them to invoice lines in one go.

Works with Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills and any one2many lines configured via XML.
