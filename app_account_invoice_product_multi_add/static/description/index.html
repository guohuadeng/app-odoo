功能概述 / Overview

本模块为客户发票（Customer Invoice）和供应商账单（Vendor Bill）提供一键批量添加产品功能。点击"Multi Add Items"按钮，在弹窗中可按分类过滤和分组产品，多选后一键批量添加到发票明细行。

This module provides one-click batch product addition for Customer Invoice and Vendor Bill. Click "Multi Add Items" to open a popup where you can filter, group and multi-select products, then batch add them to invoice lines.
